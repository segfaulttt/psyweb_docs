# 1. Authentication

## Registration

### FR-001
The platform shall allow a client to create an account.

### FR-002
The platform shall allow a specialist to submit a registration request.

### FR-003
A specialist account shall remain inactive until approved by an administrator.

### FR-004
The platform shall prevent duplicate accounts using the same email address.

## Authentication

### FR-005
The platform shall allow registered users to sign in.

### FR-006
The platform shall allow authenticated users to sign out.

### FR-007
The platform shall restrict access to protected resources for unauthenticated users.

## Authorization

### FR-008
The platform shall assign role-based permissions to authenticated users.

### FR-009
The platform shall restrict functionality according to the user's role.

---

# 2. Client Profile

### FR-010
Clients shall be able to view and edit their personal profile.

### FR-011
Clients shall be able to upload and update a profile picture.

### FR-012
Clients shall be able to manage their authentication credentials.

### FR-013
Clients shall be able to view their appointment history.

### FR-014
Clients shall be able to view their upcoming appointments.

---

# 3. Specialist Profile

### FR-015
Specialist shall be able to create and edit their public profile.

### FR-016
Specialists shall be able to upload and update profile photos.

### FR-017
Specialists shall be able to manage information about their education.

### FR-018
Specialists shall be able to manage work experience information.

### FR-019
Specialists shall be able to manage certifications.

### FR-020
Specialists shall be able to manage consultation methods.

### FR-021
Specialists shall be able to manage specializations.

### FR-022
Specialists shall be able to manage supported consultation languages.

### FR-023
Specialists shall be able to manage available consultation formats.

### FR-024
Specialists shall be able to manage consultation policies.

### FR-025
Specialists shall be able to publish or hide their public profile.

---

# 4. Services

### FR-026
Specialists shall be able to create consultation services.

### FR-027
Specialists shall be able to edit consultation services.

### FR-028
Specialists shall be able to deactivate consultation services.

### FR-029
Each consultation service shall contain:

- title;
- description;
- consultation duration;
- consultation type;
- price (optional for MVP);
- availability status.

### FR-030
Clients shall be able to view specialists services.

---

# 5. Availability

### FR-031
Specialists shall be able to create available appointment slots.

### FR-032
Specialists shall be able to remove available appointment slots.

### FR-033
The platform shall prevent creating overlapping appointment slots.

### FR-034
Available appointment slots shall become unavailable immediately after booking.

---

# 6. Search

### FR-035
Clients shall be able to browse specialists.

### FR-036
Clients shall be able to search specialists.

### FR-037
Clients shall be able to filter specialists by:

- specialization;
- consultation type;
- language;
- availability.

### FR-038
Clients shall be able to sort specialists.

### FR-039
Clients shall be able to view specialist profiles.

---

# 7. Booking

### FR-040
Clients shall be able to book available appointment slots.

### FR-041
The platform shall prevent booking occupied appointment slots.

### FR-042
Clients shall not be able to create overlapping appointments.

### FR-043
Clients shall be able to cancel future appointments.

### FR-044
Specialists shall be able to cancel appointments.

### FR-045
Specialists shall be able to view their appointment schedule.

### FR-046
The platform shall notify participants about appointment creation, cancellation and status changes.

---

# 8. Reviews

### FR-047
Clients shall be able to submit reviews after completed consultations.

### FR-048
Reviews shall only be available after consultation completion has been confirmed by both participants.

### FR-049
Clients shall be able to edit their own reviews.

### FR-050
Clients shall be able to view reviews of specialists.

### FR-051
Administrators shall be able to moderate reviews.

---

# 9. Chat

### FR-052
The platform shall support chat between specialists and clients.

### FR-053
Specialists shall be able to enable or disable chat.

### FR-054
Clients shall only be able to communicate with specialists whose chat is enabled.

---

# 10. Administration

### FR-055
Administrators shall be able to review specialists registration applications.

### FR-056
Administrators shall be able to approve specialist applications.

### FR-057
Administrators shall be able to reject specialist applications.

### FR-058
Administrators shall be able to manage user accounts.

### FR-059
Administrators shall be able to deactivate specialist profiles.

### FR-060
Administrators shall be able to view platform statistics.
