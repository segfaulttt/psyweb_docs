
# Product Decisions

**Status:** Approved

**Version:** 1.0

**Last Updated:** 2026-07-03

---

# 1. Platform Type

## Decision

The platform is designed as a marketplace connecting clients with independent psychologists.

## Rationale

Psychologists manage their own profiles, services, schedules, and consultation policies while clients can freely search and choose specialists.

---

# 2. Psychologist Verification

## Decision

Every psychologist must be verified by an administrator before becoming publicly visible.

## Rationale

Verification increases trust and prevents fake specialist profiles.

---

# 3. Booking Model

## Decision

Booking is instant.

Clients reserve available time slots directly without waiting for psychologist approval.

## Rationale

Psychologists control their schedules in advance, making additional approval unnecessary.

---

# 4. Consultation Types

## Decision

The platform supports both online and offline consultations.

The psychologist decides which consultation types are available.

---

# 5. Payments

## Decision

Payments are handled outside the platform.

The psychologist is responsible for arranging payment with the client.

## Rationale

Payment processing is intentionally excluded from the MVP.

---

# 6. Chat

## Decision

The platform provides an optional chat.

Each psychologist can enable or disable chat for communication with clients.

---

# 7. Video Consultations

## Decision

Built-in video consultations are not included in the MVP.

They are planned for future releases.

---

# 8. Reviews

## Decision

A review can only be submitted after a successfully completed consultation confirmed by both participants.

---

# 9. Availability Management

## Decision

Psychologists manage their own availability by creating and removing appointment slots.

The platform does not manage vacations or working schedules automatically.

---

# 10. Consultation Policies

## Decision

Each psychologist defines their own consultation policies.

Examples include:

- cancellation policy;
- minimum interval between sessions;
- available consultation formats;
- consultation duration;
- communication preferences.

---

# 11. Services

## Decision

Each psychologist may provide one or more consultation services.

Each service may define:

- title;
- description;
- consultation type;
- duration;
- price (optional in MVP);
- availability.

---

# 12. Future Features

The following features are intentionally excluded from the MVP:

- online payments;
- built-in video calls;
- mobile application;
- AI assistant;
- calendar synchronization;
- notification system.
