## Overview

This section defines the Booking domain of the PsyWeb system.

The Booking module owns two related but distinct lifecycle concepts:

* Reservation — a temporary hold on an AvailabilitySlot before booking confirmation;
* Booking — a confirmed consultation between a client and a specialist.

Post-session attendance and session result are modeled separately from Booking status through the SessionOutcome model.

This separation prevents temporary reservation state, appointment cancellation state, and post-session outcome from being mixed into one status enum.

---

# Core Concepts

## Reservation

A Reservation represents temporary client intent to book an AvailabilitySlot.

An ACTIVE Reservation temporarily holds the slot:

`AvailabilitySlot FREE → RESERVED`

Reservation expiration or cancellation releases it:

`AvailabilitySlot RESERVED → FREE`

Reservation confirmation creates a Booking and occupies the slot:

`Reservation ACTIVE → CONFIRMED`

`AvailabilitySlot RESERVED → BOOKED`

---

## Booking

A Booking represents a confirmed consultation created from a successfully confirmed Reservation.

A Booking does not represent the temporary hold stage.

There is no Booking before Reservation confirmation succeeds.

---

## SessionOutcome

SessionOutcome represents what happened after the scheduled consultation time.

It is separate from Booking status.

The Booking lifecycle answers:

> Does a confirmed appointment exist, or was it cancelled?

The SessionOutcome lifecycle answers:

> Did the scheduled consultation actually occur?

---

# Booking Status Model

Booking uses the following statuses:

* `CONFIRMED`
* `CANCELLED`

There is no `CREATED` Booking status.

Temporary pre-confirmation state belongs to Reservation.

There is no `NO_SHOW` Booking status.

No-show claims belong to the post-session outcome reporting model.

There is no `COMPLETED` Booking status.

Successful completion is derived from a finalized SessionOutcome whose final outcome is `OCCURRED`.

---

# Booking Status Semantics

## CONFIRMED

The Reservation has been successfully confirmed and the consultation exists.

A CONFIRMED Booking may be:

* upcoming;
* currently within its scheduled time;
* past its scheduled time while post-session outcome is unresolved;
* historical after a final SessionOutcome has been established.

Time determines whether the appointment is upcoming or past.

BookingStatus does not duplicate that temporal information.

---

## CANCELLED

The confirmed consultation was cancelled through the normal cancellation workflow.

`CANCELLED` is terminal.

A cancelled Booking is not reactivated.

If availability is released and another client later books the same AvailabilitySlot, a new Reservation and a new Booking are created.

---

# Booking Creation

A Booking is created only through confirmation of an existing ACTIVE Reservation.

Typical flow:

`ACTIVE Reservation`
→ validate ownership
→ validate Reservation is unexpired
→ validate client eligibility
→ validate specialist eligibility
→ validate AvailabilitySlot is RESERVED
→ Reservation becomes CONFIRMED
→ Booking is created with CONFIRMED status
→ AvailabilitySlot transitions `RESERVED → BOOKED`

Reservation confirmation, Booking creation, and the AvailabilitySlot transition form one atomic business operation.

No direct:

`FREE → BOOKED`

transition exists.

---

# Booking Cancellation

Cancellation is separate from post-session outcome reporting.

A normal Booking cancellation is available only before the scheduled session starts.

## Client Cancellation

When a client cancels a CONFIRMED Booking:

`Booking CONFIRMED → CANCELLED`

and:

`AvailabilitySlot BOOKED → FREE`

The slot may subsequently receive another Reservation only if it is still bookable according to its booking deadline.

Whether the cancellation is considered late is derived from `cancelledAt` relative to the configured client cancellation deadline.

Late cancellation does not introduce another Booking status.

Both normal and late client cancellations use:

`Booking.status = CANCELLED`

Cancellation metadata records the initiator and time.

Detailed booking-cutoff and cancellation policy rules are documented separately.

---

## Specialist Availability Removal

If a specialist removes availability associated with a CONFIRMED Booking:

* the Booking is cancelled consistently;
* the AvailabilitySlot transitions `BOOKED → CANCELLED`;
* the slot is not returned to FREE.

The removed AvailabilitySlot remains historical and terminal.

---

# Boundary Between Cancellation and Post-Session Outcome

Cancellation answers:

> Was the confirmed appointment cancelled before the session began?

Post-session outcome answers:

> What happened when a non-cancelled appointment reached its scheduled session time?

A correctly cancelled Booking does not enter the post-session outcome workflow.

Once the scheduled session has started, ordinary cancellation is no longer used to represent attendance or non-attendance.

Claims such as client no-show or specialist no-show belong to SessionOutcome reporting.

---

# Post-Session Outcome Model

A non-cancelled Booking becomes eligible for outcome reporting after its scheduled session has ended.

The reporting window lasts 24 hours after the scheduled session end.

Conceptually:

`reportingDeadline = slot.endTime + 24 hours`

SessionOutcome may be created when the reporting phase begins or lazily when the first report is submitted.

The exact persistence creation strategy is an implementation-level decision.

---

# SessionOutcome Resolution State

SessionOutcome has a resolution state separate from its final outcome.

## PENDING

The reporting window is open and the final result has not yet been determined.

Reports from the client and specialist may still be submitted.

---

## DISPUTED

The submitted reports conflict and cannot be resolved automatically.

Administrative resolution is required.

---

## RESOLVED

A final outcome has been established.

The final outcome is one of:

* `OCCURRED`
* `DID_NOT_OCCUR`

---

## UNVERIFIED

The 24-hour reporting window expired without a report from either participant.

The system does not infer whether the session occurred.

Properties:

* no final `OCCURRED / DID_NOT_OCCUR` outcome is inferred;
* reviews are not enabled;
* no participant is automatically penalized;
* the lack of reports is retained as historical information.

`UNVERIFIED` is terminal unless a future product requirement explicitly introduces administrative reopening.

---

# Session Reports

The CLIENT and SPECIALIST report independently.

Each participant may submit at most one SessionReport for a Booking outcome.

Submitted reports are immutable.

A report response is one of:

* `OCCURRED`
* `DID_NOT_OCCUR`

---

# DID_NOT_OCCUR Reasons

A `DID_NOT_OCCUR` report requires a structured reason.

Minimum reason values:

* `CLIENT_NO_SHOW`
* `SPECIALIST_NO_SHOW`
* `TECHNICAL_PROBLEM`
* `OTHER`

When `OTHER` is selected, a free-text explanation is required.

---

# Automatic Outcome Resolution

## Both Participants Report OCCURRED

Client:

`OCCURRED`

Specialist:

`OCCURRED`

Result:

* SessionOutcome → `RESOLVED`
* final outcome → `OCCURRED`

The consultation is considered successfully completed.

---

## Both Participants Report DID_NOT_OCCUR

For MVP, DID_NOT_OCCUR reports are automatically compatible when both participants select the same structured reason.

Result:

* SessionOutcome → `RESOLVED`
* final outcome → `DID_NOT_OCCUR`

If both reports use `OTHER`, both must provide their own explanation. The explanations do not have to be textually identical.

If the structured reasons conflict, the case becomes a dispute.

---

# Conflicting Reports

Examples:

Client:

`OCCURRED`

Specialist:

`DID_NOT_OCCUR`

or:

Client:

`DID_NOT_OCCUR / SPECIALIST_NO_SHOW`

Specialist:

`DID_NOT_OCCUR / CLIENT_NO_SHOW`

Result:

`SessionOutcome → DISPUTED`

The system does not automatically choose one participant's claim.

An administrator must resolve the dispute.

---

# Single Report at Timeout

If exactly one participant submits a report before the 24-hour deadline and the other participant does not respond:

* the reporting window closes;
* the submitted report is accepted;
* SessionOutcome becomes `RESOLVED`;
* its final outcome is derived from the submitted response.

If the submitted response is DID_NOT_OCCUR, its structured reason is retained as the accepted reported reason.

No dispute is created merely because the second participant did not respond.

---

# No Reports at Timeout

If neither participant submits a report before the reporting deadline:

`SessionOutcome → UNVERIFIED`

The platform does not assume:

* that the consultation occurred;
* that it did not occur;
* that either participant was at fault.

No review becomes available.

No automatic reliability penalty is applied.

---

# Dispute Resolution

An administrator participates only when reports conflict.

The admin does not replace the normal two-party reporting process.

Typical flow:

`conflicting SessionReports`
→ SessionOutcome becomes DISPUTED
→ admin reviews both reports and relevant context
→ DisputeResolution is recorded
→ SessionOutcome becomes RESOLVED
→ final outcome becomes OCCURRED or DID_NOT_OCCUR

The DisputeResolution remains stored as historical audit data.

---

# Review Eligibility

Review eligibility is based on the finalized session outcome, not BookingStatus alone.

A review may be created only when:

`SessionOutcome.resolutionStatus = RESOLVED`

and:

`SessionOutcome.finalOutcome = OCCURRED`

The following do not enable reviews:

* CANCELLED Booking;
* unresolved PENDING outcome;
* DISPUTED outcome awaiting admin resolution;
* finalized DID_NOT_OCCUR outcome;
* UNVERIFIED outcome.

---

# Concurrency and Transaction Rules

Reservation and Booking operations remain strongly consistent.

Critical atomic operations include:

## Reservation Creation

* create ACTIVE Reservation;
* transition AvailabilitySlot `FREE → RESERVED`.

## Reservation Confirmation

* Reservation `ACTIVE → CONFIRMED`;
* Booking creation;
* AvailabilitySlot `RESERVED → BOOKED`.

## Reservation Cancellation or Expiration

* Reservation → `CANCELLED` or `EXPIRED`;
* AvailabilitySlot `RESERVED → FREE`.

## Booking Cancellation

* Booking `CONFIRMED → CANCELLED`;
* apply the corresponding AvailabilitySlot transition.

Post-session reporting does not modify AvailabilitySlot occupancy.

---

# Domain Invariants

The Booking domain enforces the following invariants:

* a Booking is created only from a confirmed Reservation;
* a Reservation produces at most one Booking;
* Booking has no provisional CREATED state;
* a cancelled Booking cannot be reactivated;
* ordinary cancellation is separate from post-session attendance reporting;
* BookingStatus does not encode no-show or attendance outcome;
* each participant may submit at most one immutable SessionReport;
* a final OCCURRED or DID_NOT_OCCUR result is produced only through outcome resolution;
* conflicting participant claims are not resolved automatically;
* absence of both reports does not imply successful completion;
* review eligibility requires a finalized OCCURRED outcome.

---

# Summary

The Booking domain:

* owns Reservation and Booking lifecycle;
* creates Booking only after Reservation confirmation;
* uses BookingStatus only for confirmed-versus-cancelled appointment lifecycle;
* keeps post-session result separate from BookingStatus;
* collects independent CLIENT and SPECIALIST reports;
* resolves compatible reports automatically;
* accepts a single submitted report after the reporting timeout;
* marks sessions with no reports as UNVERIFIED;
* routes conflicting claims to administrative dispute resolution;
* enables reviews only after a finalized OCCURRED outcome;
* preserves historical Reservation, Booking, report, outcome, and dispute data.
