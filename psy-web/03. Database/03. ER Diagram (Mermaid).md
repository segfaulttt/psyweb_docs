# ER Diagram

## Entities

| Entity | Primary Key | Foreign Keys | Fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| **User** | `id` | — | `email`, `password_hash`, `role`, `status`, `created_at` |
| **Specialist** | `user_id` | `user_id → User.id` | `first_name`, `last_name`, `approval_status`, `minimum_booking_notice` |
| **Service** | `id` | `specialist_id → Specialist.user_id` | `title`, `description`, `duration_minutes`, `price`, `is_active` |
| **AvailabilitySlot** | `id` | `specialist_id → Specialist.user_id` | `start_time`, `end_time`, `status`, `minimum_booking_notice` |
| **Reservation** | `id` | `client_id → User.id`, `slot_id → AvailabilitySlot.id` | `status`, `created_at`, `expires_at`, `cancelled_at`, `cancellation_initiator` |
| **Booking** | `id` | `reservation_id → Reservation.id`, `client_id → User.id`, `specialist_id → Specialist.user_id`, `slot_id → AvailabilitySlot.id`, `service_id → Service.id` | `status (CONFIRMED / CANCELLED)`, `created_at`, `cancelled_at`, `cancellation_initiator` |
| **SessionOutcome** | `id` | `booking_id → Booking.id` | `resolution_status (PENDING / DISPUTED / RESOLVED / UNVERIFIED)`, `final_outcome (OCCURRED / DID_NOT_OCCUR, nullable)`, `reporting_deadline`, `finalized_at` |
| **SessionReport** | `id` | `session_outcome_id → SessionOutcome.id`, `author_id → User.id` | `response (OCCURRED / DID_NOT_OCCUR)`, `reason`, `details`, `created_at` |
| **DisputeResolution** | `id` | `session_outcome_id → SessionOutcome.id`, `admin_id → User.id` | `decision (OCCURRED / DID_NOT_OCCUR)`, `comment`, `resolved_at` |
| **Review** | `id` | `client_id → User.id`, `specialist_id → Specialist.user_id` | `rating`, `comment`, `created_at`, `updated_at` |
| **SpecialistModerationAction** | `id` | `specialist_id → Specialist.user_id`, `admin_id → User.id` | `action`, `reason`, `created_at` |
| **ChatThread** | `id` | `booking_id → Booking.id`, `client_id → User.id`, `specialist_id → Specialist.user_id` | `created_at` |
| **Message** | `id` | `thread_id → ChatThread.id`, `sender_id → User.id` | `text`, `sent_at` |

### Derived Attributes

`AvailabilitySlot.duration` is not stored separately.

It is calculated as:

`end_time - start_time`

## Relationships

| Relationship | Cardinality | Description |
|---|---|---|
| User → Specialist | `1 : 0..1` | A user may have zero or one Specialist profile |
| Specialist → Service | `1 : *` | A specialist may offer multiple services |
| Specialist → AvailabilitySlot | `1 : *` | A specialist may create multiple availability slots |
| User (Client) → Reservation | `1 : *` | A client may create multiple reservations |
| AvailabilitySlot → Reservation | `1 : *` | A slot may have multiple historical reservations, but only one ACTIVE reservation at a time |
| Reservation → Booking | `1 : 0..1` | A reservation may produce at most one booking |
| User (Client) → Booking | `1 : *` | A client may have multiple historical bookings |
| Specialist → Booking | `1 : *` | A specialist may have multiple historical bookings |
| AvailabilitySlot → Booking | `1 : *` | A slot may have multiple historical bookings because cancelled bookings may be followed by rebooking |
| Service → Booking | `1 : *` | A service may be referenced by multiple bookings |
| Booking → SessionOutcome | `1 : 0..1` | A booking may have at most one session outcome |
| SessionOutcome → SessionReport | `1 : 0..2` | A session may have at most one report from each participant |
| SessionOutcome → DisputeResolution | `1 : 0..1` | A session outcome may have at most one administrative dispute resolution |
| User (Client) → Review | `1 : *` | A client may review multiple specialists |
| Specialist → Review | `1 : *` | A specialist may receive multiple reviews |
| Booking → ChatThread | `1 : 0..1` | A booking may have at most one chat thread |
| ChatThread → Message | `1 : *` | A chat thread contains multiple messages |
| Specialist → SpecialistModerationAction | `1 : *` | A specialist may have multiple historical moderation actions |
| User (Admin) → SpecialistModerationAction | `1 : *` | An admin may perform multiple moderation actions |

## Relationship Constraints

### User ↔ Specialist

`User 1 ─── 0..1 Specialist`

`Specialist.user_id` is both:

- the primary key of `Specialist`;
- a foreign key referencing `User.id`.

A Specialist does not have an independent primary key.

---

### Specialist ↔ AvailabilitySlot

`Specialist 1 ─── * AvailabilitySlot`

Each availability slot belongs to exactly one specialist.

A specialist may create multiple slots.

---

### AvailabilitySlot ↔ Reservation

`AvailabilitySlot 1 ─── * Reservation`

A slot may have multiple historical reservations.

Only one reservation may be `ACTIVE` for a slot at a time.

Historical `EXPIRED` and `CANCELLED` reservations remain stored.

---

### Reservation ↔ Booking

`Reservation 1 ─── 0..1 Booking`

A reservation may result in at most one booking.

The relationship is enforced by a unique constraint on `Booking.reservation_id`.

---

### AvailabilitySlot ↔ Booking

`AvailabilitySlot 1 ─── * Booking`

A slot may have multiple historical bookings because a cancelled booking may be followed by a new reservation and booking.

Only one active booking may occupy a slot at a time.

---

### Booking ↔ SessionOutcome

`Booking 1 ─── 0..1 SessionOutcome`

A non-cancelled Booking may have at most one SessionOutcome.

Booking status and post-session outcome are independent concerns.

Booking status is limited to the confirmed/cancelled appointment lifecycle.

Attendance and session result are represented by SessionOutcome.

A CANCELLED Booking does not enter the normal post-session outcome workflow.

---

### SessionOutcome ↔ SessionReport

`SessionOutcome 1 ─── 0..2 SessionReport`

Each participant may submit at most one report:

- CLIENT
- SPECIALIST

Reports are immutable after submission.

Each report contains:

- `OCCURRED`; or
- `DID_NOT_OCCUR` with a structured reason.

`OTHER` requires free-text details.

The reporting window closes 24 hours after the scheduled session end.

---

### SessionOutcome ↔ DisputeResolution

`SessionOutcome 1 ─── 0..1 DisputeResolution`

Conflicting participant reports transition the SessionOutcome to `DISPUTED`.

Only disputed outcomes require administrative resolution.

The administrator resolves the dispute to:

- `OCCURRED`; or
- `DID_NOT_OCCUR`.

After administrative resolution:

- `resolution_status = RESOLVED`;
- `final_outcome` contains the decision;
- the DisputeResolution remains stored for audit history.

If exactly one participant reports before the reporting deadline, that report is accepted after timeout.

If neither participant reports, the SessionOutcome becomes `UNVERIFIED` and no final outcome is inferred.

---

### Client ↔ Specialist Review

A client may create at most one review for a particular specialist.

The existing review may be edited later.

This is enforced by:

`UNIQUE(client_id, specialist_id)`

A review may only be created for a session whose final outcome is `OCCURRED`.

---

### Specialist ↔ SpecialistModerationAction

`Specialist 1 ─── * SpecialistModerationAction`

Moderation actions are historical records.

Suspension or rejection does not delete the specialist or their historical data.

---

### Booking ↔ ChatThread

`Booking 1 ─── 0..1 ChatThread`

A booking may have one associated chat thread.

The chat thread and its messages are separate from the booking lifecycle.

## Core Cardinality and Consistency Constraints

- `User.email` must be unique.
- `Booking.reservation_id` must be unique.
- `Specialist.user_id` is a shared primary key and foreign key to `User.id`.
- A slot may have many historical reservations, but only one `ACTIVE` reservation at a time.
- A reservation may produce at most one booking.
- A slot may have many historical bookings, but only one active booking at a time.
- Cancelled bookings may be followed by rebooking.
- A booking may have at most one SessionOutcome.
- A SessionOutcome may have at most one report from each participant.
- A SessionOutcome may have at most one DisputeResolution.
- A client may have at most one Review for a particular specialist.
- Historical reservations, bookings, session outcomes, reviews, and moderation actions are not deleted as part of normal lifecycle transitions.
- Booking status does not encode post-session attendance or no-show outcome.
- A non-cancelled Booking may have at most one SessionOutcome.
- A SessionOutcome may have at most one immutable SessionReport from each participant.
- A RESOLVED SessionOutcome has final outcome OCCURRED or DID_NOT_OCCUR.
- DISPUTED outcomes require administrative resolution.
- If exactly one participant reports before timeout, that report determines the final outcome after the reporting window closes.
- If neither participant reports, the outcome becomes UNVERIFIED and no successful completion is inferred.
- Review eligibility requires a RESOLVED SessionOutcome with final outcome OCCURRED.
